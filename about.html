How I work

Discovery with the client

Start with the people who do the work. Find the real problem, what it costs, and what “good” looks like, before choosing a model or a framework.

Small, evaluated iterations

Ship a thin working slice, measure it against labeled cases, and let the results decide the next change. Every change runs against the same tests.

Honest metrics

Report what was measured, including the failures and the times a metric itself was wrong. Projections are labeled as projections.

Security by default

Secrets out of code, document text treated as data rather than instructions, cost caps, and a human in the loop before anything acts on the outside world.

Clean handoff

Architecture notes, decision records and runbooks, so the client's own team can run and change the system without me.
