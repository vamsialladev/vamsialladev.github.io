Life

More than a job title.

I grew up in Andhra Pradesh, India, and studied there: a B.Sc., then an MCA at Acharya Nagarjuna University. In 2014 I wrote my first production Java code, and I've been building software ever since. Today I work remotely from the US.

Family comes first for me. Most of what I do, at work and outside it, starts with wanting to build something steady for the people I care about.

Travel keeps me curious. A new place is a lot like a new client: you learn the most when you listen first.

Cricket is my sport. I'll happily talk through a good match with anyone who'll let me.

And when I want to switch off, it's Telugu and Indian movies, and the music that stays in my head for days afterwards.
